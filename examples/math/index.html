The identity eiπ+1=0

Inline formulas sit in the line like words: for any real θ, the point eiθ lies on the unit circle, at angle θ from the real axis. Written out,

eiθ =cosθ+isinθ

and at θ=π the sine vanishes and the cosine is −1, which is

eiπ+1=0

Its title rides in the running head of every page of this chapter as a formula, set a step smaller by math-depth.A footnote can hold a formula too: ∫0πsinxdx=2. The browser sets it at the note's size.
